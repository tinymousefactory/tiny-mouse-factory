About Tiny Mouse Factory and Yuki Nishiyama.
A small studio working across documentary film, video, and music.

Tiny Mouse Factory

Yuki Nishiyama
Yuki Nishiyama

FILM DIRECTOR / FILMMAKER / COMPOSER / DRONE OPERATOR

Tiny Mouse Factory is the creative studio of filmmaker and composer Yuki Nishiyama.
I chose the name because I believe that even a small operation — perhaps especially a small one — can make meaningful work.

Born in Ako District, Hyogo Prefecture, Japan, in 1981.
At 21, I left university to pursue composition.
After working in composition, arranging, and music education, I began professional photography and cinematography at 31.
My work includes video production, cinematography, drones, VR/360° filmmaking, and editing for national parks, museums, local governments, and businesses, alongside music composition and arranging.

In 2019, I joined the filming crew for the National Museum of Nature and Science's Recreating a 30,000-Year-Old Voyage project, contributing to film, VR, and television production.
There I met Jomon carpenter Kunihiro Amemiya. In 2025, I released my directorial debut, the documentaryMinna: A Boat for Everyone.
My next film,Forging a Prayer – Voice of the KATANA, follows Bizen Osafune swordsmith Kazuki Kawashima through a year of making a votive sword.
I also appear regularly as a guest on FM Setagaya's Maika's Wonderful Friends.

Lectures & Talks

Lectures and talks

I speak about creative work, craftsmanship, cultural encounters, and the experiences of filmmaking, music, and taking on new challenges.

“To Create Is to Live”

A journey from music to video and film, and what it means to keep making things while changing forms of expression.

“From a 30,000-Year-Old Voyage to Today”

The connections between people and cultures revealed through the making and voyage of a dugout canoe.

“Stories of People and Culture Through Film”

The lives encountered through documentary filmmaking, and why documenting them matters.

“Beyond Turning a Passion into a Career”

Experiences of work and creativity, from composer and arranger to filmmaker and director.

Speaking engagements can also be arranged through the speaker agency S-Enterprise.
