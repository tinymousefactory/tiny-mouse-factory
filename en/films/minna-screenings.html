Minna: A Boat for Everyone

Past Screenings

Thanks to the generous support of organizers and audiences, Minna: A Boat for Everyone was presented at independently organized screenings for more than a year, through May 2026. We are sincerely grateful to everyone who made these events possible.

Since June 2026, screenings have been available through cinemo, a community screening platform. We invite you to consider organizing a screening through cinemo.
